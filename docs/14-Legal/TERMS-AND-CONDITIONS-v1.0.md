## 1. Who these terms apply to

SearchMyVacation, trading as **Search My Vacation**, provides travel planning, coordination and travel services. These Terms and Conditions apply when you ask Search My Vacation to plan, quote, arrange, coordinate or sell travel services for you.

Please read them carefully before accepting a quotation or asking us to confirm travel services. Where a supplier’s terms apply to a service, those terms also apply and may contain additional conditions, cancellation charges or limitations.

## 2. Our role

Search My Vacation acts as a travel planner, coordinator and seller of travel services. We help understand your travel needs, prepare options, coordinate services, provide quotations and, when you authorise us, arrange confirmed travel services.

Some travel services are delivered by independent suppliers. Search My Vacation may arrange those services on your behalf or sell them as part of an agreed travel arrangement, while the relevant supplier remains responsible for delivering its own service.

## 3. Journey Passport and Journey Director

The Journey Passport helps you share what matters for your journey. It is not a booking request, reservation, quotation, confirmation or guarantee of availability.

Journey Director recommendations and a Journey Synopsis are advisory planning tools. They are designed to support an informed conversation with a human Journey Designer; they do not confirm routes, inventory, prices, visas, weather, supplier availability or inclusions.

No travel service is booked or held unless Search My Vacation confirms it in writing after receiving the required payment or deposit and supplier confirmation where applicable.

## 4. Quotations and validity

Unless a quotation states otherwise, the following Release 1 validity periods apply from the time the quotation is issued:

| Travel start date | Quotation validity |
| --- | --- |
| Travel begins in less than 7 calendar days | 24 hours |
| Travel begins in 7–13 calendar days | 72 hours (3 days) |
| Travel begins in 14–29 calendar days | 5 calendar days |
| Travel begins in 30 calendar days or more | 10 calendar days |

The following rules apply to every quotation:

- A quotation is not a reservation or confirmation.
- Hotels, transport, flights and other travel services are not held unless Search My Vacation explicitly confirms this in writing.
- Prices and availability remain subject to change until the required deposit or payment has been received and the booking has been confirmed.
- If a supplier specifies a shorter validity period, the supplier’s validity will prevail.
- Promotional offers, limited-inventory properties and special fares may expire earlier than the standard validity periods.
- Search My Vacation reserves the right to revise or withdraw a quotation before confirmation if there is a material change in supplier pricing, availability, taxes, government levies, exchange rates, or any other factor outside its reasonable control. Where possible, we will notify you promptly and provide an updated quotation.

## 5. Availability, pricing and confirmation

Inventory is not held unless Search My Vacation confirms it in writing. Availability, rates, taxes, government levies, exchange rates, airline rules and supplier conditions can change before confirmation.

A booking is confirmed only when Search My Vacation gives written confirmation after the required deposit or payment has been received and the relevant supplier confirmation has been obtained where applicable. Until then, a proposed journey remains subject to availability and price change.
