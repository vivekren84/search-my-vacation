# 1. Purpose

This document records the Product Owner Review conducted for the Notifications module as part of Release 1.3.

The review established Notifications as the Workspace capability responsible for drawing attention to business conditions that require awareness or action. It also clarified the distinction between Notifications and Tasks and approved the governance principles that prevent notifications from becoming a substitute for operational work management.

These notes preserve the approved Product Owner decisions and governance principles for future Product Specification updates.

---

# 2. Module Vision

## Approved Vision

Notifications provide Workspace Users with timely awareness of operational events and business conditions requiring attention.

Notifications improve operational responsiveness by ensuring important information is surfaced proactively rather than requiring users to discover issues manually.

The module supports awareness and operational visibility but does not replace task management.

---

# 3. Business Purpose

The Product Owner approved the following business purpose.

Notifications exist to:

- provide timely operational awareness,
- highlight business conditions requiring attention,
- reduce the risk of missed operational activities,
- support operational decision-making,
- improve Workspace responsiveness.

Notifications are intended to encourage Workspace Users to make the Workspace their primary operational environment.

---

# 4. Primary Business Object

## Notification

The Notification is the primary business object.

A Notification represents a system-generated indication that a business condition exists.

Notifications are informational objects.

They do not represent ownership of work.

---

# 5. Product Owner Decisions

## PD-NO-001 – System Generated

**Approved**

Notifications shall always be generated by the Workspace.

Users shall not manually create notifications.

Manual reminders are handled through Tasks.

---

## PD-NO-002 – Separation from Tasks

**Approved**

Notifications and Tasks represent different business concepts.

Notifications provide awareness.

Tasks represent work to be completed.

The Workspace shall maintain these as separate capabilities.

---

## PD-NO-003 – Notification Types

**Approved**

Notifications are classified into two categories.

### Informational

Provides awareness only.

Examples include:

- Journey successfully confirmed
- Destination Profile approved
- Vendor activated

Informational notifications may be acknowledged once reviewed.

---

### Action Required

Represents a business condition requiring attention.

Examples include:

- Traveller documents outstanding
- Vendor quotation overdue
- Departure approaching with incomplete readiness
- Review awaiting approval

Action Required notifications remain active until the underlying business condition has been resolved.

---

## PD-NO-004 – Resolution Behaviour

**Approved**

Notifications shall not disappear merely because a user has viewed or acknowledged them.

A Notification remains active until the underlying business condition has been resolved.

The Product Owner determined that dismissing notifications without resolving the associated business issue undermines operational effectiveness.

---

## PD-NO-005 – Workspace Awareness

**Approved**

Notifications exist to encourage Workspace Users to engage with the Workspace daily.

Operational awareness is considered a key characteristic of the Workspace experience.

---

# 6. Business Object Structure

## Identity

Each Notification contains:

- Notification ID
- Notification Type
- Source Module
- Creation Date
- Current State

---

## Context Information

Each Notification records:

- related business object,
- originating module,
- notification message,
- severity,
- creation timestamp.

---

## Business Relationship

Notifications may reference:

- Traveller
- Journey Planning Record
- Journey
- Vendor
- Destination Profile
- Task
- Workspace User

Notifications do not own these objects but provide awareness of their state.

---

# 7. Governance Decisions

## Awareness Versus Work

The Product Owner approved a clear distinction between:

Notification

↓

Awareness

Task

↓

Work

The Workspace shall not use notifications as substitutes for operational task management.

---

## Condition-Based Lifecycle

Notification lifecycle is determined by the underlying business condition.

Viewing a notification does not resolve it.

Business resolution closes the notification.

---

## Organisational Consistency

Notifications shall be generated consistently across Workspace modules.

Notification behaviour remains independent of individual module implementation.

Notifications establish a consistent awareness layer across the Search My Vacation Workspace.
