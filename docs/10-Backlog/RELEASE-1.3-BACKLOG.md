## Purpose and How to Read This Document

This document is a **capture and categorisation record**, not a scoped, sequenced release plan. Unlike `RELEASE-1.2-BACKLOG.md` (which pre-scoped Release 1.2 into P1/P2/P3 candidates before workstreams were finalised), this document does not assign a priority ranking to any item beyond the categorisation below — that scoping exercise happens later, when the Product Owner is ready to formally open Release 1.3 planning, in the same pattern `EBC-R1.2-WS6-01` established for Workstream 6 (product discovery before delivery planning).

Every item below is either a **decision** (has an identifiable decision-maker and approval) or a **vision item** (an approved direction for future capture, not yet a decision to build). The two are kept visually and structurally distinct throughout this document so that neither is mistaken for the other.

## 2. Google Ads Conversion Tag — Updated Product Owner Direction (23-Aug-2026)

This entry supersedes the "Tier C — requires reconciliation" treatment this item received in the original `EBC-R1.2-WS6-04` consolidation. The Product Owner has since given explicit direction, recorded here in full rather than paraphrased down:

- **The Google Ads Conversion Tag integration is a planned Release 1.3 feature.**
- **Integration point:** at the successful completion of the Journey Passport flow, immediately before the traveller transitions to Journey Director or WhatsApp.
- **This commitment is subject to the normal Release 1.3 architecture and implementation review** — it is a planning commitment, not a guarantee that precedes Archie's and Rad's normal review discipline.

**Reconciliation with the prior WS4 Product Review Gate record.** `DR-R1.2-WS4-001` v1.0 (DR-WS4-005) recorded the Product Owner's earlier position that Google Ads conversion tracking was "not committed to a specific release." This document's Section 2 records the Product Owner's superseding, more specific direction of 23-Aug-2026, per this project's Source of Truth precedence (latest explicit Product Owner instruction outranks a prior decision record). `DR-R1.2-WS4-001` is not edited or deleted — per this project's decision-governance convention, a superseded decision is marked, not rewritten — but Tiger records here, for anyone reading `DR-R1.2-WS4-001` in isolation, that its DR-WS4-005 release-timing position has been superseded by this document's Section 2.

## 10. UX Improvements

Captured from **WS5 Engineering Review 1** — Task 7 (`EBC-R1.2-WS5-REV1-07-RAD`), Task 8 (`EBC-R1.2-WS5-REV1-08-RAD`), and Task 9 (`EBC-R1.2-WS5-REV1-09-RAD`) — and formally deferred to Release 1.3 per `DEC-R1.2-024`/`DEC-R1.2-025` (`docs/10-Backlog/RELEASE-1.2.md` §7), `EBC-R1.2-WS5-GOV-05-TIGER-Tasks7-8-Governance-Synchronization`, `EBC-R1.2-WS5-GOV-05A-TIGER-Governance-Amendment` (Product Owner disposition of `OBS-7-01`), and `EBC-R1.2-WS5-GOV-06-TIGER-Engineering-Review1-Closure-and-Governance-Synchronization`. Items in this section are traveller-facing interaction/copy improvements, distinct in kind from Section 9's engineering technical debt, and are recorded separately by deliberate choice — see `GOV-05`'s Activity 2.3 (`OBS-8-01`), `GOV-05A`'s Activity 1 (`OBS-7-01`), and `GOV-06`'s Activity 3 (`OBS-9-02`) for the rationale in each case. One further entry below — **Journey Passport Cross-Step Intent Synchronization** — was not sourced from WS5 Engineering Review 1 at all; it was raised directly by the Product Owner during exploratory validation conducted after the Review's closure, and is recorded per `EBC-R1.2-WS5-GOV-06A-TIGER-Governance-Amendment` (Amendment 01 to `GOV-06`).

**Journey Passport Recovery Experience.**

- **Source:** `OBS-7-01`
- **Owner:** Sophie (UX Design); Rad (Engineering implementation)

Review and improve the traveller recovery experience following successful OTP verification but unsuccessful lead creation. Objectives include: eliminating the retry dead-end (today, re-pressing "Verify code" after a failed `/leads` submission replays the same, already-consumed token and reproduces the identical failure indefinitely, per `OBS-7-01`'s evidence); providing clear recovery guidance; directing travellers toward the correct recovery action ("Resend code," the one action that actually works, currently unmentioned by the failure message); reducing confusion following transient backend failures; and maintaining a smooth Journey Passport completion experience throughout. Classified, per Product Owner approval, as a Release 1.3 UX Improvement — explicitly not Engineering Technical Debt and not an Architecture item; no engineering implementation is required for Release 1.2.

**Journey Director Recovery Messaging.** Improve traveller guidance following a Journey Director initialization failure to ensure recovery actions are clear and consistent. If the recommendation engine throws on the traveller's first arrival at Journey Director and the traveller then reloads the page while trying to recover, the in-memory passport handoff is lost (it is written to `sessionStorage` only after the recommendation engine has already succeeded once) and a different, more confusing "no completed travel story for this visit" message is shown instead of the original, more accurate "your possibilities need a little more care" message — even though the underlying lead remains safely persisted throughout. A future scoping pass should consider having the passport handoff also write a minimal, passport-only entry to `sessionStorage` at the point of arrival, distinct from the full session entry written only on a successful recommendation, so a reload during this narrow window restores the original, accurate message rather than the generic one. Cross-reference: `OBS-8-01`.

**OTP Response Recovery.**

- **Source:** `OBS-9-02`
- **Owner:** Sophie (UX); Rad (Engineering)

Improve traveller recovery when the initial OTP response is lost after a successful send, ensuring clear guidance and graceful recovery without requiring discovery of manual workarounds. Today, if the response to the first successful "send code" call is lost in transit, the client never captures a usable challenge ID; a resubmission within the resend cooldown window is silently treated as "proceed to the code-entry screen" regardless of whether a working challenge ID exists, so the traveller lands on that screen unable to verify, with no indication that "Change number" — the one control that actually recovers the flow — is the way out. A working recovery path already exists in the interface; it simply isn't surfaced at the moment it's needed.

**Journey Passport Cross-Step Intent Synchronization.**

- **Source:** Product Owner exploratory validation, conducted after WS5 Engineering Review 1 closure (not sourced from an `OBS-x-xx` engineering observation)
- **Owner:** Sophie (UX Design); Arjun (Product Analysis); Rad (Engineering)
- **Priority:** Medium

During post-closure exploratory validation of the Journey Passport, the Product Owner observed that traveller selections made in earlier steps are not visibly reflected in later steps. For example, a traveller who selects Mood = "Celebrations" and Dream Journey = "Wildlife" sees only the Mood selection influence what follows at Pace & Timing; the Dream Journey selection is not visibly carried forward. The current implementation is functioning exactly as designed — this is **not a defect** — but the effect is that the Journey Passport can feel like a sequence of independent questions rather than a connected, personalised conversation.

This item enhances the Journey Passport so that traveller intent gathered in earlier steps is meaningfully reflected in later steps, through contextual recommendations, intelligent defaults, and progressive personalization. Possible areas include: Mood → Pace & Timing, Dream Journey → Pace & Timing, Companion → Recommended Experiences, and earlier traveller preferences informing later suggestions more broadly. **Guiding Principle:** improve continuity and personalization without automatically overriding traveller choices. Classified, per Product Owner decision, as a Release 1.3 UX Improvement — explicitly not a defect and not Engineering Technical Debt; no engineering implementation is required for Release 1.2. Recorded per `EBC-R1.2-WS5-GOV-06A-TIGER-Governance-Amendment` (Amendment 01 to `EBC-R1.2-WS5-GOV-06-TIGER-Engineering-Review1-Closure-and-Governance-Synchronization`).
